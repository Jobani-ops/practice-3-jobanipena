Mondrian trained as a traditional landscape painter.

Around 1908 he began to simplify, and his famous tree paintings show the process step by step, a real tree breaking down into a web of lines until the branches become a flat pattern. By about 1920 nothing was left of the tree. Only horizontal and vertical lines, white space, and the three primary colors plus black, white and grey.
